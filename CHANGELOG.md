# Clicky

## 1.2.0

- **Favorite Mounts and Favorite Pets are new, and Favorites is now Favorite Toys.** Star mounts
  and pets in their journals or in Clicky's grid, then drag Favorite Mounts or Favorite Pets to a
  bar for a random favorite. If you already put Favorites on a bar, it keeps that name.
- **Playing on more than one computer? Update Clicky on all of them.** An older Clicky doesn't
  know Favorite Mounts and Favorite Pets, so clicking them there does nothing.
- **The drawer shows how many you carry** of a thing from your bags, like a stack of flasks, when
  you have more than one.
- **Fixed: the search could miss words in a tooltip,** like a toy's "Use:" line, when the game
  hadn't loaded them yet. Now the results update once they arrive.

## 1.1.1

- **Fixed: clicking a macro did nothing when it picked a pet.** The drawer worked, but the random
  click didn't. Now it summons the pet, in combat too.
- **Gift-wrapped mounts and pets show up as gifts in the grid,** like they do in the journals.
  Click one to unwrap it, same as a toy.
- **NEW only shows on things you haven't unwrapped yet.** Toys you learn without a gift wrap don't
  get it anymore.

## 1.1.0

- **Give a macro the icon of one of its items.** Click the macro in the panel to show its items,
  right-click the one you want and choose **Use as icon for** your macro.
- **Open a macro in `/macro` from the panel.** Right-click it and choose **Open in /macro** to
  edit it there.
- **Right-clicking an item under a macro in the panel is about that macro now.** It used to show
  the star and every macro; now it offers **Use as icon for** and **Remove from** that macro. Use
  the grid to star things or add them to other macros.

## 1.0.1

- **Clicky is on CurseForge now,** as well as Wago, so addon managers that use CurseForge can
  install and update it.
- **Clicky is open source,** under the MIT license.

## 1.0.0

- **Clicky's first release.** Put toys, mounts, pets, spells and items together in a macro: click
  its button for a random one, or right-click it to pick one from its drawer.
- **Favorites** is a macro of the toys you star in the Toy Box.
- **Clicky's grid takes the Toy Box's place,** with a tab each for Toys, Mounts, Pets, Spells and
  Items, its own search, and the Filter menus of the Toy Box and the journals. Your own filters in
  those windows stay as you left them.
- **Your macros are ordinary WoW macros,** so they work on every computer you play on, and updates
  won't break them.
- **Buttons and drawers work in combat.**
- **`/clicky debug` opens a window you can copy from,** for a bug report.
