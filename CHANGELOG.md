# Clicky

## 0.6.0 (alpha)

- **Macros from Clicky 0.4.0 and 0.5.0 can't be read.** They're greyed out with "?" and use
  nothing. Delete them and make them again.
- **A Favorites button from Clicky 0.4.0 on a bar stops working.** Drag Favorites from the panel to
  the bar again. The old macro is left in /macro for you to delete.
- **Update Clicky on every computer you play on.** Older versions of Clicky can't use macros this
  version makes.
- **A macro holds up to 100 items, down from 150 toys.** Items are toys, spells, and things in
  your bags or that you wear, in any mix.
- **What a patch adds shows in the grid once Clicky is updated for that patch.** Until then a new
  toy, spell or item isn't listed, so you can't add it to a macro.
- **Spells and items can go in your macros too.** The grid's Spells and Items tabs list the spells
  you know and the items in your bags or worn. Right-click one to add it to a macro, as with a toy.
  Try a Portal macro with portal toys, "Path of the ..." spells and a teleport cloak.
- **Clicky's grid takes the place of the Toy Box's,** with a tab each for toys, spells and items.
  Your favorite toys come first, then the rest by name, in one list of icons you scroll. Click,
  drag or shift-click as in the Toy Box.
- **Gear that can be worn is only in the drawer.** A click on a macro never picks it, because WoW
  wears gear that isn't worn instead of using it. Right-click the button and choose it there: if
  you aren't wearing it, choosing it puts it on, and choosing it again uses it.
- **A check mark shows which toys, spells and items are in your macros.** Right-click one to tick
  the macros it's in; the menu used to say Add to and Remove from. Favorites' toys have the Toy
  Box's star instead.
- **The Toys tab has its own filters:** Collected, Not Collected and Usable Only. Your Toy Box
  filters aren't changed.
- **Fixed: once you delete Favorites' macro, Favorites is called Favorites again.** It used to
  keep the deleted macro's name until you logged in again.

## 0.5.0 (alpha)

- **Your macros keep their toys through patches.** Clicky now has its own list of every toy, so a
  patch can no longer leave a macro unreadable.
- **New toys need a Clicky update.** Once Clicky is updated for a patch, you can add that patch's
  toys to macros, and Favorites uses them when you star them.
- **Toys removed from the game stay in your macros.** Clicky used to drop them; now you can remove
  them yourself in the panel.
- **Your macros update when you log in.** They keep their toys, and your action bars keep working.
  Update Clicky on every computer you play on: Clicky 0.4.0 can't use macros from this version,
  including Favorites. If a macro can't be updated, it's greyed out; delete it and make it again.
- **If you pasted a Clicky macro's /click line into a macro of your own, copy it again.** The old
  line no longer works. Right-click the macro in the Clicky panel and choose **Copy /click line**.
- **Fixed: drawers show every toy's icon the first time they open.** They used to show black
  squares for toys the game hadn't finished loading.
- **Fixed: a new macro gets its toy's icon** even if the game hadn't finished loading the toy. It
  used to get the Favorites chest.

## 0.4.0 (alpha)

- **Drawers:** right-click any Clicky button on your bars for a drawer of that macro's toys, with
  their cooldowns. Click a toy to use it.
- **Combat:** macros and their drawers now work in combat.
- **Favorites** can now be renamed and deleted like your own macros.
- **Your own macros:** right-click a macro in the panel and choose **Copy /click line** to use its
  toys from a macro of your own.
- The panel lists every toy in a macro; toys you don't own are greyed out, and you can remove them.
- Clicking a macro with no toys says how to add some.
- Other windows, such as `/macro`, now make room for the Clicky panel instead of opening under it.

## 0.3.0 (alpha)

First test release.

- A random-toy button for your Toy Box favorites.
- Your own random-toy macros: right-click a toy in the Toy Box and choose **Add to new macro...**,
  then add more toys from the same menu.
- A panel beside the Toy Box lists your macros and their toys. Drag a macro from it to your bar.
- Macros work on every computer you play on.

This is an alpha: things will change, and macros made now may need to be made again later.
